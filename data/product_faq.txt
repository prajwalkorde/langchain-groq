Product FAQ

Q: What does InsightPilot do?
A: InsightPilot turns internal notes, reports, and FAQs into searchable answers.

Q: Who is it for?
A: It is for analysts, operations teams, and founders who need quick answers from company documents.

Q: Does it replace analysts?
A: No. It helps analysts find relevant context faster and draft first-pass answers.

Q: What is the biggest implementation risk?
A: Poor document quality. If source documents are outdated, the system may produce weak answers.

Q: What is a good first use case?
A: A support knowledge base or internal operations manual is usually a good first use case.
