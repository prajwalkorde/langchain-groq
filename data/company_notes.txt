Acme Analytics is a fictional data consulting company.

Its main services are dashboard development, data pipeline design, customer segmentation,
sales forecasting, and AI-assisted reporting.

The company prefers practical AI features that reduce repetitive analyst work.
It avoids projects where the business problem is unclear or the data quality is unknown.

Acme's ideal clients are small and mid-sized companies with existing data but limited
internal data engineering capacity.

Common project risks include vague stakeholder goals, missing source-system documentation,
overly broad dashboard requests, and dashboards without adoption plans.
